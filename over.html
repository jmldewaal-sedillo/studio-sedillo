About Studio Sedillo

Studio Sedillo makes small, friendly games and apps, for children and for grown-ups.

How we work

  • No accounts, no tracking, no statistics. What a game saves stays on your own device.
  • Games for children have no advertising and no purchases.
  • Every game has its own privacy policy and a page that explains how to delete your data.
