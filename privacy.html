Hosting

This website is hosted by GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc.) and is only served over an encrypted connection (HTTPS). Like any web server, it may briefly record technical data such as the IP address when it sends you the pages. GitHub uses this to deliver the pages and to keep the website secure; the legal basis is our legitimate interest in a website that works and is safe (Article 6(1)(f) GDPR). We have no access to data that identifies you.

If you e-mail us

Our e-mail runs through Gmail, a service of Google. If you e-mail us, your e-mail address and your message are stored with Google; see the Google Privacy Policy. We use them only to answer your question. The legal basis is our legitimate interest in answering questions that are sent to us (Article 6(1)(f) GDPR). We do not pass your message on to anyone else and delete it within one year after your question has been dealt with.

GitHub (hosting) and Google (e-mail) are American companies, so the technical data and e-mails mentioned above may be processed in the United States. Both companies take part in the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework, which the European Commission has recognised as giving adequate protection. Their privacy statements: GitHub and Google.
